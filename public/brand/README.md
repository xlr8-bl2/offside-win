# The mark

The "o" of offside and the ball: a white ring and a violet dot, on the page
colour. It is the wordmark reduced to two shapes: the wordmark is "offside",
a violet ball for the full stop, and "win" in the quieter colour, set in
Geist at its heaviest and tracked tight (`.wordmark` in base.css).

It replaced an italic O with the broadcaster's offside line through it and
a football beyond. That mark, and the wordmark it matched ("off|side" in the
condensed display face with the line through the word), read as a cursor or
a typo at small sizes and said the same thing twice when they sat together.

Drawn as shapes, not type, so it is the same wherever the font is not
loaded. The PNGs are rendered from `../icon.svg`; the maskable and Apple
ones put the mark inside the middle 80% of a full-bleed square.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `mark.svg` | the mark on its own, full detail |
| `logo-120.png` | Google's sign-in branding (square, 120 px) |
| `logo-1024.png` | anywhere that wants a large square logo |
| `../icon.svg`, `../icon-32.png` | browser tab |
| `../icon-192.png`, `../icon-512.png` | home-screen icon (rounded tile) |
| `../apple-touch-icon.png`, `../icon-512-maskable.png` | full-bleed, for systems that cut their own shape |
| `../og.png` | the link preview |

Colours are the site's own: chalk `#f4f6fa`, floodlight violet `#9e86ff`,
ground `#0a0a0c`. Flat everywhere: no glow, no gradient, at any size. The
owner's call.

**The percent sign is on purpose.** O, line, ball reads as "%", a nod to
confidence levels. Do not "fix" it by moving the ball or breaking the
diagonal.
