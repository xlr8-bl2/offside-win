# The mark

An italic O, the offside line, and the ball beyond it. Drawn by the owner;
traced from that drawing (the O and the line), with the ball rebuilt as a
true football pattern so its seams are clean at every size.

How the drawing is built, so it stays that way:
- The bar cuts through a dark channel on both sides. The O stops 24.5
  units short of the bar's left edge; the ball is cut in a straight line
  19.5 units beyond its right edge. Both cuts run parallel to the bar.
- The ball's dark panels are holes: they show the background, so they are
  drawn in the ground colour, never a colour of their own.
- The ball faces a white hexagon, slightly up and right of centre, with
  its three dark panels upper-left (into the cut), upper-right (to the
  edge) and at the bottom.

Colours are the site's own: chalk `#f7f4ed`, floodlight violet `#7a5af8`,
ground `#0c0b09`. Flat everywhere: no glow, no gradient, at any size. The
owner's call.

**The percent sign is on purpose.** O, line, ball reads as "%", a nod to
confidence levels. Do not "fix" it by moving the ball or breaking the
diagonal.
